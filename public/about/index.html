Current Members#

Hayward Godwin#

I am currently an Associate Professor and have been working at the University of Southampton for some time now, and live in Bournemouth with my wife and children. Most of my research has focused on search and attention, using a variety of different approaches (standard behavioural experiments, eye-tracking, online experiments). In more recent years I’ve branched out in a number of different ways, including working on some open science / metascience projects as well as clinical projects. Throughout my career I’ve engaged in a mix of both basic and applied research, primarily involving tasks which involve search, such as airport baggage screening.

I am currently the Director of the Psychology BSc course in our department; prior to this, I had the same role overseeing the MSc Psychology course. At present, I don’t have a great deal of teaching, but get to supervise dissertation students and teach the Open Science in Psychology MSc course module.

A full list of my publications can be found here.
