It can be difficult finding where in a journal article the authors have let the reader know what scientific outputs have been shared alongside that journal article. This is primarily because where the outputs are linked to and shared is inconsistent between journal articles and journals themselves.

Output it Forward (OIF for short) is a Chrome extension that helps with this. By pressing the OIF button in your toolbar, it can scan the entire page you have loaded in your web browser for common words, phrases, and links to popular output sharing repositories (such as the OSF). OIF can also scan through a PDF if you have that loaded in your web browser instead.

After it scans whatever is in your browser tab, it will then provide a pop-up to help you see what links to shared outputs have been detected. Alongside that, it will highlight in yellow in the loaded article itself where the outputs have been found.
