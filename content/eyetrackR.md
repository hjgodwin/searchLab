Processing data from eye-tracking experiments can be difficult and time-consuming. The goal of *eyeTrackR* is to provide you with a set of tools and functions that can make it easier. EyeTrackR uses R and is available via CRAN to download.

It has primarily been tested and used for experiments that involve visual search, scene perception, or other similar types of display. The functions provided by eyeTrackR focus on processing the reports generated by SR Research's Data Viewer software, which ties in with their EyeLink eye-trackers. 

You can download eyeTrackR directly from R/Rstudio by clicking on 'Packages' and searching for eyeTrackR.
